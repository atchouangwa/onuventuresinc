About

A Dallas firm building where others won’t.

We identify overlooked opportunities and transform them into places built for long-term value.

Founded in Dallas in 2021, Onu Ventures sources, underwrites, capitalizes, develops, and constructs residential and mixed-use projects in growing Texas markets. The firm works where private capital has historically been absent, building the housing and neighborhood retail those communities have gone without.

Our thesis is straightforward: rigorous market work, disciplined execution, and a long hold view produce assets that hold their value and communities that keep their character.

Mikial Onu

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

A Houston native, Mikial Onu earned a BBA at Southern Methodist University and an MS at the University of Colorado, playing football for both schools before a broken tibia ended a professional career. He began his working life in insurance brokerage, moved into real estate, and founded Onu Ventures in Dallas in 2021.

He was named Emerging Leader in D CEO’s Commercial Real Estate Awards, and has been recognized on D CEO’s Dallas 500 list of the region’s most influential leaders in consecutive years.
